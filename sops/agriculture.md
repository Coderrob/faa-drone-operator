# Agriculture SOP

- Coordinate landowner/tenant, field boundaries, workers, livestock, roads, irrigation wires and obstacles.
- Expect low-level agricultural aircraft; monitor seasonal activity and yield/land early.
- Account for heat, dust, remote terrain, weak communications, large-area VLOS and battery recovery.
- Mapping follows the photogrammetry SOP with crop-specific lighting/ground-control needs.
- Dispensing or applying substances can require Part 137 and federal/state pesticide,
  environmental and worker-protection compliance; basic Part 107 is not sufficient.
- Maintain chemical separation from imaging equipment/batteries and follow applicable SDS/site controls.
