# Public-Safety SOP

- Identify operating authority: Part 107 versus public-aircraft/COA framework;
  document the chosen framework and do not mix privileges casually.
- Integrate incident command, air operations coordination, crew accountability,
  evidence rules, cybersecurity, records requests and agency policy.
- Check emergency/VIP/wildfire TFRs and coordinate through authorized channels;
  emergency purpose does not automatically waive flight rules.
- Establish manned-aircraft deconfliction, lost-link containment, public perimeter,
  sensitive-image access and live-stream recipients.
- For evidence, preserve original files, metadata, hashes/access log and chain of custody.
- Abort or transfer control when incident workload exceeds crew capacity or aviation safety.
