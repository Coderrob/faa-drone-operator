# Infrastructure Inspection SOP

- Obtain owner/site safety authorization; coordinate energized equipment, lockout,
  rail/road, confined-space, fall and industrial hazards with responsible personnel.
- Identify wires, magnetic/RF interference, GNSS multipath, thermal plumes,
  repetitive/reflective surfaces and turbulent structure flow.
- Set standoff, zoom, route, alternate landing and lost-link behavior that cannot
  cause automatic collision with the asset.
- Use a payload operator when detailed observation would cause screen fixation.
- Abort on unstable positioning, link/GNSS warning, personnel entry or inadequate standoff.
- Preserve defect location/orientation and image traceability; distinguish observation
  from engineering conclusion and route findings to a qualified professional.
