# Mapping and Photogrammetry SOP

- Define required ground sample distance, horizontal/vertical accuracy, coordinate
  reference system, deliverables and whether licensed surveying is implicated.
- Choose altitude, speed, shutter, overlap/sidelap and terrain strategy from actual requirements.
- Establish surveyed ground control and independent checkpoints when required;
  checkpoints are not fitting control points.
- Automated grids remain inside VLOS, authorization, people/vehicle and battery limits.
- Control lighting/shadows, rolling shutter, wind blur, reflective/water/texture-poor surfaces.
- Validate coverage in field; process with documented CRS, camera model and settings.
- Report checkpoint residuals, gaps and limitations. A clean-looking model is not proof of accuracy.
