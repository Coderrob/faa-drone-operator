# Construction and Real-Estate SOP

- Obtain owner/general-contractor permission and coordinate daily site plan.
- Treat workers as nonparticipants unless they are directly participating under
  the rule; ordinary notice alone does not make every worker a participant.
- Control cranes, lifts, haul routes, adjacent traffic, dust, changing wires and launch access.
- For progress series, fix coordinate references, route, altitude, time window,
  camera settings and naming while still adapting for safety.
- For property media, document boundaries, neighboring privacy and image-use approval.
- Abort for equipment entering containment area, crane movement conflict, dust/VLOS loss or traffic.
