# Photography and Video SOP

- Obtain site/subject permissions and define privacy, release, retention and delivery terms.
- Scout sun, reflections, background, subject/vehicle movement, wires and bystanders.
- Design shots that avoid flight over nonparticipants and moving vehicles unless eligible.
- Separate camera operator when framing workload would impair aircraft scanning.
- Remote PIC announces `abort`/`traffic`; the shot never delays yielding or landing.
- Lock exposure/color only when appropriate; verify focus, motion blur, storage and timestamps.
- Abort for subject incursion, VLOS degradation, fixation, unexpected traffic or energy limit.
- Postflight validate representative files before leaving; preserve flight/permission records.
