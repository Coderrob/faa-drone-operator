### Operations over people and moving vehicles

Part 107 subpart D contains Category 1–4 eligibility and operating conditions,
labeling/documentation, maintenance, and moving-vehicle rules. Use the current
[subpart D text](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/part-107/subpart-D) with the
aircraft's FAA-accepted Declaration of Compliance and operating instructions.
Never infer Category 2/3 eligibility from weight or marketing alone.

### Waivers

[§107.200](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-107.200) establishes
waiver policy and [§107.205](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-107.205)
lists waivable rules. Retain the issued waiver and build a provision-by-provision
compliance matrix. A waiver is neither airspace authorization nor site permission.

## Per-mission rule audit

- [ ] Record applicable rule/authorization/waiver, not merely “Part 107.”
- [ ] Link each special operation to aircraft eligibility and documents.
- [ ] Recheck eCFR effective date and FAA guidance revision.
- [ ] Carry certificate, photo ID, registration, and issued approvals as required.
- [ ] Preserve authorization/waiver conditions with the mission record.
- [ ] Record why OOP, moving-vehicle, night, RID, and airspace gates pass.
