# Part 107 Flight Desk

Part 107 Flight Desk is a source-grounded learning and operations workspace for
FAA remote pilots. It brings the certification process, ACS knowledge,
interactive practice, field procedures, and continuing-compliance records into
one maintained project.

## Start here

1. Follow the [certification roadmap](01-certification-roadmap.md).
2. Complete the [registration processes](06-registration-processes.md).
3. Work through the [ACS study guide](02-acs-study-guide.md).
4. Use the [14-day study plan](03-study-plan.md).
5. Adopt the [field-operations process](04-field-operations.md) before flying.

The interactive study cards and mock exams are available on the
[Part 107 Flight Desk website](https://faadroneoperator.com/).

!!! warning
    This independent study aid does not replace current FAA regulations,
    authorizations, waivers, NOTAMs, local requirements, or manufacturer
    instructions. Verify controlling information before every operation.
